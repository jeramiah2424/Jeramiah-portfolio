Project overview

Built a workflow to support invoice reporting by processing invoice information and organizing qualifying records in Google Sheets.

What I worked on

  • Configured workflow activities to retrieve invoice files and extract invoice information.
  • Used confidence checks and conditional logic to flag records that may need manual review.
  • Applied a due-date condition based on the project requirement.
  • Used AI content generation to create concise purchase descriptions.
  • Mapped invoice number, supplier, due date, total amount, and purchase description into Google Sheets.
  • Tested workflow steps and troubleshot data mapping and duplicate-row behavior.

What this project demonstrates

Foundational RPA workflow development, document data extraction, AI-assisted text generation, conditional logic, Google Sheets integration, testing, and troubleshooting. No performance or time-savings figures are claimed because they have not been measured.
